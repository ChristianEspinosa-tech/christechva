What happened, and why
Revenue per recipient is climbing
The VIP early-access test and a stronger welcome flow are the two biggest drivers — both are now outperforming last month's baseline and are worth scaling.
Deliverability dipped mid-month
A short dip around Oct 16–19 tracked with a spike in sends to the lapsed segment. It's since recovered, but worth monitoring before the next high-volume send.
New subscribers are outpacing engagement
List growth is healthy, but the new-subscriber segment isn't converting to "active" as quickly as prior cohorts — the welcome flow may need another touchpoint.
Recommendations for November
01
Roll out the winning offer framing
Apply the dollar-off framing from the October test across all abandonment sends, not just the test segment.
02
Add SMS to the abandonment flow
Currently email-only — adding a same-day SMS touch is projected to lift cart recovery meaningfully based on the current test.
03
Launch a targeted re-engagement send
The at-risk segment is 15% of the list and growing — a dedicated campaign now is cheaper than a win-back later.
04
Suppress the lapsed segment from bulk sends
Protects deliverability ahead of the holiday send volume increase in December.
