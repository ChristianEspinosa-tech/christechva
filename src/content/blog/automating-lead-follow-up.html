Automating Lead Follow-Up Without a Sales Team

Most small teams lose deals not because the leads are bad, but because nobody replies fast enough. A simple automation closes that gap: capture the lead, enrich it, and start the follow-up sequence within seconds.

The stack

  • n8n — orchestration and routing logic
  • GoHighLevel — CRM, SMS and email sequences
  • Google Sheets — lightweight reporting layer

What changes

Response time drops from hours to under a minute, every lead gets the same structured follow-up, and the owner only steps in when someone replies.
