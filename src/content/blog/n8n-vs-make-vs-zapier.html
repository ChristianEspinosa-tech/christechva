n8n vs Make vs Zapier: Picking the Right Tool

There is no universally best automation platform — only the right fit for the workload, budget and level of control you need.

Quick guide

  • Zapier — fastest to ship simple, linear automations.
  • Make — visual branching and high-volume scenarios at lower cost.
  • n8n — self-hosted, custom code, AI agents and full data ownership.

For most client builds I start in n8n, because the same workflow can grow from a two-step trigger into a full AI agent without a migration.
